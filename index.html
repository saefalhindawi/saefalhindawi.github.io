Samsung.com · GEO · AEM

Samsung Product Care page, built from scratch for people and AI search

Problem
More customers now ask AI assistants instead of browsing support pages. Traditional article pages were hard for AI engines to read and quote correctly.
What I did
Designed and built the Samsung Levant Product Care & Proactive Tips page in Adobe Experience Manager (AEM), user-friendly and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) friendly:
  • An "In short" answer summary at the top that AI engines can quote directly
  • Interactive product hotspots: select a part to see its care tips
  • A care schedule (every use, monthly, when needed) and navigation by symptom (drainage, odor, noise, errors)
  • 20 structured FAQs marked up with FAQPage schema, plus "Reviewed by Samsung Support" and last-reviewed date for trust
Tools
Adobe Experience Manager, HTML, Schema.org structured data, GEO / SEO
Result: One page that guides customers step by step and that AI search engines can read, trust, and cite.

Generative AI · Prompt engineering · Low budget

2 AI assistants for call agents, built with free Google NotebookLM

Problem
A low-budget client had no enterprise AI or knowledge platform, but its agents still needed fast, accurate answers during live customer calls.
What I did
Turned the free Google NotebookLM into 2 generative AI assistants:
  • Built a clean, well-structured knowledge base to use as the assistants' only source
  • Wrote the prompts that make each one act as a support assistant: its role, tone, what to answer, and what not to answer
  • Tested the answers, then rolled both assistants out to agents for use on live calls
Tools
Google NotebookLM, knowledge base design, prompt engineering, RAG
Result: 2 AI assistants in daily use on calls, giving agents answers from the client's own approved content, at no software cost.
